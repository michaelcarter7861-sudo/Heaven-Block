Privacy Policy & Data Safety

HavenBlocks: Cozy Room & Garden (Package: com.havenblocks.cozyroom) · Version 1.0.0

At a glance: HavenBlocks is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not require user accounts, do not collect personal profiles, and store your save progress strictly on your own device.

1. Data Stored On Your Device

Your puzzle progress, Cozy Room furniture, Herbarium cards, Journal milestones, Cozy Stars, Cozy Gems, and settings are saved locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage. No game saves are uploaded to external developer servers.

You may export a portable offline backup code or restore a previous backup code anytime from Settings → Backup / Restore Progress.

2. Optional Rewarded Ads & Consent (Android / Google Play)

HavenBlocks does not display forced interstitial popups during gameplay. On Android Google Play builds, optional opt-in rewarded ads (for extra Tactical Undo, Rotate, Revive, Double Stars, or Free Gems) may be served via Google AdMob.

  • Regional consent (including EEA/UK GDPR) is requested and managed via Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • You can review or update your ad consent choice anytime inside Settings → Ad Privacy & Consent (UMP).
  • Purchasing the optional No-Ads Starter Bundle removes all rewarded ad waits.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

All optional bundles and Cozy Gem packs on Android are processed directly by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store credit card numbers or billing credentials. Non-consumable entitlements (such as the No-Ads bundle) can be restored via Settings → Restore Purchases.

4. How to Delete Your Data

Because HavenBlocks stores game data exclusively on your device without any cloud account, you can erase all local game data instantly at any time by:

  • Opening Settings → Delete Local Save Data inside the app and confirming the reset; or
  • Clearing app storage or uninstalling HavenBlocks from your Android device settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

HavenBlocks does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children or adults. No social chat, user-generated uploads, or account registration exist in the app.

6. Contact & Support

For privacy questions, Play Store support, or purchase assistance, please contact the app publisher through the developer support email listed on the official HavenBlocks Google Play Store listing.
